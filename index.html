Mission

The force's last line of digital defense.

The Directorate protects command networks, runs signals intelligence for operations, and hunts adversaries before they reach critical systems. Every officer here is selected for judgment under pressure, not just technical knowledge.

Defend

Harden and monitor command infrastructure against intrusion.

Collect

Gather and process signals intelligence in support of operations.

Hunt

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I see my score after submitting?

No. The terminal only confirms transmission and gives you a submission reference. Command reviews scores and domain breakdowns directly.

What happens if the timer runs out?

Your responses are submitted automatically. Unanswered items are scored as incorrect.

Can I go back and change answers?

Yes. Use the item navigator or flag items for review. Once you transmit, your responses are sealed.

What if I fail?

You may reapply in the next recruitment cycle. Command may also release you early for integrity events.
